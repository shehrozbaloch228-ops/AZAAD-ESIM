Before you order

Will it work on your phone?

Check this before you buy. Whether it works depends on your device and network.

Quick check: dial this code

*#06#

  1. Open the phone dialer and enter the code above. If an EID number is listed, your phone has eSIM hardware.
  2. Or open Settings and look for "eSIM" or "Add eSIM" in the mobile or SIM settings. The menu name differs between brands.
  3. On iPhone, open Settings, General, About. "Carrier Lock" should say "No SIM restrictions".

An EID only shows that the phone has eSIM hardware. It does not tell you the phone is unlocked.

Your phone needs to be:

  • eSIM-capable
  • Factory unlocked
  • Not a JV or carrier-locked phone. We do not support JV phones.

iPhone XR and newer, many recent Samsung Galaxy S and Z models and Google Pixel phones usually support eSIM. It varies by model and region, and some phones sold in mainland China have no eSIM.

Ask on WhatsApp
What is Azaad eSIM?

Azaad eSIM is a data-only eSIM package for compatible Non-PTA phones in Pakistan. It gives your phone mobile internet. It does not come with a phone number.

Will it work on my phone?

Your phone needs to support eSIM, be factory unlocked, and not be a JV (carrier-locked) device. Whether it works also depends on the device and network, so check before you buy. If you are not sure, send us your phone model on WhatsApp.

Can I make calls or send SMS with it?

No. The eSIM is data only, so there is no phone number, calling or SMS. You can use apps such as WhatsApp, FaceTime or Zoom over the data connection.

Do I keep my own SIM?

Yes. Keep your physical SIM for your own number, calls and OTP messages. Use the Azaad eSIM only for mobile data.

Does this register my phone with PTA?

No. Azaad eSIM does not register your phone with PTA and does not change its PTA status. It is a data-only service and does not replace PTA registration.

How do I order and pay?

Choose a plan and tap Order. The order screen shows our SadaPay account details. Transfer the total amount, then tap Confirm Order & Send Screenshot on WhatsApp and attach your payment screenshot in the chat. Our team confirms your order and sends your QR code.

How do I get the QR code?

After your payment is checked and your order is confirmed, we send your eSIM QR code by WhatsApp or email. Scan it from your phone's eSIM settings to install the eSIM.

What if it does not work?

We have a money-back guarantee. If your phone is supported and factory unlocked, and the eSIM does not connect because of a technical error on our side, our team will refund you.

What is a JV phone?

JV phones are carrier-locked phones. We do not support them. Carrier-locked phones usually cannot install eSIM profiles from other providers. Your phone must be factory unlocked and support eSIM.
