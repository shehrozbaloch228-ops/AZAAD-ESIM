Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers about how Azaad eSIM interacts with your device.

What exactly is Azaad eSIM?
Azaad eSIM is an international, data-only profile. It allows compatible smartphones in Pakistan to access internet services through data roaming agreements, routing traffic independently of local carrier restrictions.
Can I make regular cellular calls or receive local SMS?
No. Azaad eSIM provides data only. It does not come with a local phone number. You must use apps like WhatsApp, FaceTime, or Zoom for calls and messages. Your physical SIM will continue to handle incoming OTPs and standard SMS depending on your device's network status.
Does this register my phone with the PTA?
No. This service does not pay taxes or officially register your IMEI with the PTA. It acts purely as a roaming layer to provide internet connectivity, without modifying your official registration status.
How do I purchase and activate a plan?
Select a plan from our pricing section and click 'Complete Order'. This will prepare an order summary and redirect you to WhatsApp. Send the generated message to our support team to complete payment and receive your activation QR code.
What is your refund policy if it does not work?
We offer a reliable money-back guarantee. If you own a supported, factory-unlocked device and the eSIM fails to establish a data connection due to technical errors on our end, our support team will process a prompt refund.
