Questions, answered

Know what you are ordering.

Read the essentials about data-only service, compatibility, payment and delivery before you place an order.

Is Azaad eSIM a normal phone SIM?
Azaad eSIM is a data-only service. It does not provide a phone number, regular calls or SMS.
Can I keep my own SIM in the phone?
Yes. Keep your own SIM for your existing phone number, calls and SMS if your phone supports dual SIM use.
Does Azaad eSIM register my phone with PTA?
No. Azaad eSIM is a data-only service. It does not register your phone with PTA.
How do I order?
Choose a plan, transfer the total amount to the SadaPay details shown in the order screen, then confirm on WhatsApp with your payment screenshot.
How will I receive the eSIM?
After your order is confirmed, we send your QR code. Scan it from your phone’s eSIM settings.
Can I use Azaad eSIM on a JV phone?
Your phone must be eSIM-capable and factory unlocked. JV or carrier-locked phones may not support the service.
Can I make calls or send SMS with Azaad eSIM?
No. Azaad eSIM is data only and has no phone number, calls or SMS. Internet apps can use the data connection.
What happens if my phone is not compatible?
Check eSIM support, factory-unlocked status and that the phone is not JV or carrier locked before buying.
Is there a money-back guarantee?
Money-back eligibility depends on the confirmed order conditions and compatibility requirements. Contact Azaad eSIM on WhatsApp before ordering if you are unsure about your phone.
